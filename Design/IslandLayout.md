# Island Layout

This document describes the physical layout of Lumen Island.

---

# World Layout

The Heartlands form the center of the island. Every region connects naturally through paths, rivers, forests, and coastlines. Players gradually restore the island as Hope grows.

---

# Island Shape

- Medium-sized island
- Irregular coastline
- Northern rocky cliffs
- Southern sandy beaches
- Central hills
- Natural rivers flowing toward the sea
